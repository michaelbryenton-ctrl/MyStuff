# XRoar — CoCo in your hands

## Treatment
16:9 landscape, 25 seconds. Promise: classic computers live on an Android phone, with a choice of touch or physical controls. Audience: the project's creator and retro-computing enthusiasts.
Identity: supplied red pixel XRoar icon; editorial cream #f4efdd, charcoal #141817, phosphor green #9fe759 and signal orange #ff6942. These are film colors, not claimed official brand tokens. Space Grotesk display and JetBrains Mono supporting type.
Hero: an illustrated phone using the project's receiver-style CoCo bezel. Signature details: silver rectangular housing, inset green CRT, black right-side speaker grille, circular tuning controls and red power light. It is artwork, not a runtime screenshot or captured gameplay.
Motion: pixel-grid assembly, macro-to-wide camera moves, mask reveals, animated cable/stick diagrams. Arc: fast nostalgic opening, slower emulator reveal, file montage, control demonstration, resolved identity. Payoff: orange field and large wordmark replace the feature compositions.
Sound: one original instrumental chiptune/synthwave bed, no narration or sound effects. Beat alignment is not claimed.

## Variety / accuracy review
Five distinct fields/compositions: dark kinetic macro, cream asymmetric phone, green media montage, dark split input diagrams, orange identity. Type and objects change meaningfully throughout each scene; final title gets over two seconds of readable screen time. Heroes occupy 60–85% of short edge. No saving/export feature, release claim, invented game footage, bundled ROM claim or universal gamepad compatibility claim. All interface representations explicitly illustrated. One persistent logo owner.
