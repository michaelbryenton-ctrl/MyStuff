## Full-screen display with compact controls

- Tap **Full screen** to enlarge the game picture while preserving its 4:3 aspect ratio, without stretching or cropping.
- Keep a small strip below the picture with **Normal**, **Keyboard**, **Enter**, and **More**.
- Use **More** for Space, straight quote, input/port/gamepad settings, Files, Library, and Exit app.
- Return to the standard layout with **Normal** or Android Back after dismissing the keyboard.
- Includes an updated seven-page user guide.

## Downloads and installation

Download `xroar-1.0.10-debug.apk` under **Assets**. This is a debug APK installed outside Google Play; Android may ask you to allow installation from the app opening it.

**Existing users: install over the previous version. Do not uninstall or clear app data**, which deletes imported files and local saved cassettes. The package and signing identity match 1.0.9, with versionCode increased to 11.

No ROMs or games are included. Supply your own legally obtained firmware and software.
